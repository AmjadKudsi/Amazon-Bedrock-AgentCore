Query: What is Nimbus Assist?
Answer: Nimbus Assist is an AI-powered support agent designed to reduce human ticket volume by providing fast, accurate, and citeable answers to customer questions using curated internal knowledge. It is intended to deliver answers with top 1-3 citations, ensuring compliance and traceability. The system is built using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with Bedrock Knowledge Bases, and it includes guardrails for handling personally identifiable information (PII) and unsupported topics. Nimbus Assist is multilingual, primarily supporting English with fallback options for Spanish and German. It operates with a high availability service level objective (SLO) of 99.9% and ensures data security through KMS encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit.
