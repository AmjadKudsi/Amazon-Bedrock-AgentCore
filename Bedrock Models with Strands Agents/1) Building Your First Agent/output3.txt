Ginny Weasley deserved so much better on screen! Here's how I'd reimagine her role to match her book brilliance:

## Early Foundation
**Establish her fierce spirit from the start.** In Chamber of Secrets, show glimpses of her personality beyond being Tom Riddle's victim - perhaps a scene where she stands up to older students picking on first-years, or demonstrates magical talent that impresses even Hermione.

## The Quidditch Revelation
**Make her athletic prowess a major plot point.** Show Ginny absolutely dominating on the Quidditch pitch - diving through impossible angles, scoring spectacular goals. Let the audience gasp at her natural talent, making it clear she's not just Harry's love interest but a formidable witch in her own right.

## Verbal Sparring Partner
**Give her the razor-sharp wit from the books.** Ginny should be lobbing clever comebacks and calling out Harry's brooding tendencies. Picture her rolling her eyes at his melodrama: "Oh please, Harry, you're not the only one who's faced Voldemort. Some of us just don't write poetry about it."

## The Hexing Prodigy
**Showcase her magical creativity.** Include scenes of Ginny inventing curses, her Bat-Bogey hex becoming legendary among students. Show her teaching other DA members spells even Hermione doesn't know.

## Emotional Intelligence
**Make her Harry's emotional anchor.** Rather than just standing around looking pretty, have Ginny be the one who actually understands Harry's psychological struggles - because she's been possessed by Voldemort too. She should be offering genuine insight, not just sympathy.

## Independent Agency
**Give her her own subplot.** Perhaps she's leading resistance efforts at Hogwarts during Deathly Hallows, or investigating mysterious magical phenomena. Let her be a protagonist in her own story, not just a satellite character.

The key is showing, not telling, that Ginny Weasley is a force of nature who chose Harry Potter - not the other way around.
